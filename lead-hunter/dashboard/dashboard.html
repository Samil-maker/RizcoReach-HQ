Settings

Who fits

A business fits when it has no website, or its website is down, parked ("coming soon") or weak: not secure, not made for phones, or scoring under the bar below. A business with a good website does not fit. Businesses with proof they just opened (Google's own label, their own ad or Instagram post, a brand new Instagram, or your own say) come first, and the cold email file says "new business".

Areas

Every kind of business is searched in these areas, top to bottom, unless it has its own list. One per line, the way you would type it into Google Maps.

Kinds of business

One per kind of business, built in so there is nothing to import. Searches run as "search term area". An exclude word drops any business with it in its name or Google category (start a line with category: to only check the category). Leave Areas empty to use the list above. A listing Google marks as new is kept whatever its review count.

Checking businesses

Instagram

Lead Hunter never opens Instagram for this. It asks Meta's Instagram Graph API (Business Discovery) for a business's bio (often with their email or WhatsApp), the link in it and their latest posts, which say whether the account is real, active, or just opened. It needs an access token from your own Meta app, once.

  1. Open the Graph API Explorer and pick your app.
  2. Under Permissions add instagram_basic, instagram_manage_insights, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and business_management, then press Generate Access Token and allow it.
  3. Copy the token, paste it below and press Connect. Lead Hunter finds your Instagram business account id itself, and swaps a short token for a long lived one when you add your app id and secret.

Cold email

Lead Hunter finds the businesses and their emails. The cold email system on your Mac (cold-email/ in the repo) writes the emails, sends them, follows up and reads the replies. Press Export for cold email on the Email leads tab and save the file in your RizcoReach Leads folder: it goes in within the hour. A business with proof that it just opened is marked "new business" in the file.

Contacted memory

Everyone you mark contacted or export for cold email is remembered here, even after you delete the lead. Searches skip them and matching leads are hidden (tick Show contacted before to see them). Import your own list too: any CSV with phone numbers.
