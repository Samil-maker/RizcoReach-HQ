Lead Hunter

Find businesses that are already spending on ads.

Lead Hunter pulls advertisers from the Meta Ad Library, checks the website their ads send people to, finds a phone number, and writes the pitch for you.

  1. Start a hunt. Pick a niche (say, car detailing) and a country. A Facebook Ad Library tab opens and scrolls through the results for every keyword.
  2. Leads get checked automatically. Each advertiser's landing page is audited for speed, mobile, Meta Pixel, contact buttons and more. Phone numbers come from WhatsApp ads, the website, and Google.
  3. Pitch the hottest ones first. Sort by priority, open a lead, and send a personalised WhatsApp or email in one click.

New hunt

Lead Hunter opens the Meta Ad Library, searches each keyword, and scrolls until it has collected your target number of new advertisers. Keep the tab it opens on screen while it runs.

Niche presets

Only advertisers with active ads are collected. Everyone found is saved; the filters decide which ones count towards your target.

Tips

  • Any Ad Library page you browse is captured too, even without a hunt. Search, scroll, and the leads show up here.
  • Use 4 to 8 related keywords per niche. Different businesses use different words ("ceramic coating" finds people "car detailing" misses).
  • "Running at least 30 days" finds steady spenders with budget. "At most 14 days" finds businesses that just started advertising and may not have a good site yet.
  • Facebook may slow you down if you scroll thousands of ads in one go. A few hunts a day is plenty.

Phone queue

Send leads to your phone and work through them anywhere: each one opens WhatsApp on your phone with the message ready, and "sent" syncs back here. Without this you can still use the QR codes in each lead and in Phone mode.

  1. Press Generate a key, then Copy key.
  2. In Netlify: Site configuration → Environment variables → add LEAD_QUEUE_KEY with that key. Then Deploys → Trigger deploy.
  3. Press Save & test connection here.
  4. Scan the pairing code below with your phone and open the link. On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen so it opens like an app.

Scan with your phone's camera to open your outreach queue. Anyone with this code can see your queue, so don't share it.
