Lead Hunter

Find businesses that are already spending on ads.

Lead Hunter finds UAE businesses on Google Maps, Instagram and the Meta Ad Library, checks their website and their ads, scores each one, and hands you a clean list for Google Contacts or a spreadsheet.

  1. Find businesses. On the Meta ads tab pick a preset and press Find businesses, or start a hunt in the Ad Library here.
  2. Leads get checked automatically. Websites are audited, Meta ads are looked up, and phone numbers come from Google, the website and WhatsApp links. Each lead gets a heat score.
  3. Export the best ones. Press Google Contacts (or Full CSV), import the file at contacts.google.com, and reach out from your phone. Exported numbers are remembered, so they never come back.

New hunt

Lead Hunter opens the Meta Ad Library, searches each keyword, and scrolls until it has collected your target number of new advertisers. Keep the tab it opens on screen while it runs.

Niche edit the list in Settings

Only advertisers with active ads are collected. Everyone found is saved; the filters decide which ones count towards your target.

Tips

  • Any Ad Library page you browse is captured too, even without a hunt. Search, scroll, and the leads show up here.
  • Use 4 to 8 related keywords per niche. Different businesses use different words ("ceramic coating" finds people "car detailing" misses).
  • "Running at least 30 days" finds steady spenders with budget. "At most 14 days" finds businesses that just started advertising and may not have a good site yet.
  • Facebook may slow you down if you scroll thousands of ads in one go. A few hunts a day is plenty.

Intent Radar

Reddit is read through Reddit's API, one request per subreddit, 4 to 12 seconds apart. Without an app id it uses Reddit's public JSON for the same pages, which is fine for a few requests every few hours. If Reddit starts refusing, create an app at reddit.com/prefs/apps (type: installed app, the id is under its name) and paste the id here. A refusal pauses Reddit and alerts you.

Keywords: a post with any of these phrases is saved under that kind. Arabic phrases about websites or marketing count as those too.

AI check

An AI reads every post the keywords catch and says whether the person is really a buyer, what they need, what business they are and where, and how urgent it is. Posts it rules out are hidden (tick Show not buyers to see them). Use a free Google AI Studio key: aistudio.google.com > Get API key > Create API key. On Google's free tier there is a daily limit (the check waits and carries on when it is reached), and Google may use what is sent to improve its products; these are public posts. Or use a Claude key (console.anthropic.com > API keys, about 0.2 US cents a post). If both are filled in, Gemini is used. Keys stay in this browser and are only sent to Google or Anthropic.

Heat score

Every lead gets one score from 0 to 100, ranked highest first. A business with a website is scored for the Ads offer, one without a working website for the Website offer, and the bonuses at the bottom add to either. Use a minus number to push a signal down. Businesses running ads are hidden by default (tick Show skipped to see them).

Quality signals

Signals shown on every lead and used for scoring. A business with fewer Google reviews than the limit is a new business.

Website checks: each one that fails adds its points to the weakness score (at most 100) and is saved as a reason. The failed check with the most points is the lead's top reason, written in its Google Contacts notes.

Instagram followers (official API)

Lead Hunter never opens Instagram for this. It asks Meta's Instagram Graph API (Business Discovery) with your own token, and it can only see business and creator accounts. You need an Instagram business or creator account linked to a Facebook page, and a Meta app. In the Graph API Explorer pick your app, add the permissions instagram_basic, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and business_management, press Generate Access Token, then run me/accounts?fields=instagram_business_account: the id it shows is your account id. Tokens from the Explorer stop working after about an hour; swap it for a long lived one (about 60 days) with the Access Token Debugger's Extend button. If the token stops working, lookups pause and you get an alert.

Presets

One per kind of business. Searches run as "keyword area", one keyword at a time across every area, in the order you list them. Paste lists one item per line. An exclude word drops any business with it in its name or Google category. Start a line with category: to only check the category. The review range keeps small, owner run businesses: listings outside it are never opened or saved.

Email sender

Sends your emails from your own Gmail, even with Chrome closed. It starts with 15 a day and goes up over two weeks. It stops when someone replies and never emails anyone twice.

The email sender needs your site and its key.

  1. Open script.google.com and sign in with the Gmail you want to send from.
  2. Click New project.
  3. Delete what's there and paste the script.
  4. Click + next to Services, pick Gmail API, then Add.
  5. Pick setup at the top and press Run, then Allow. Google warns the app isn't verified because it's your own script: click Advanced, then Go to.
  6. Done. It checks every 5 minutes and only sends Mon to Fri 9 to 6 Dubai time.

Each lead gets the emails for its offer: the Website offer when they have no working website, the Ads offer when they have one and are not running Meta ads. Leads running ads with a working website get no emails. The follow ups go as replies in the same thread. Variables: {{business}} {{niche}} {{city}} {{found_on}} {{website_line}} {{my_name}} {{agency}} {{my_phone}}
