Lead Hunter

Businesses with no website, or a weak one.

Lead Hunter finds UAE businesses on Google Maps and Google, works out whether their website is missing, dead or weak, and finds their email, phone and Instagram.

  1. Find. On the Find tab pick a kind of business (or All types) and press Find businesses. Google Maps and Google open in background tabs and are read like you would read them.
  2. Email leads are the ones with an email that passed its checks: Export for cold email and save the file in your RizcoReach Leads folder.
  3. WhatsApp & Instagram leads are the ones with a phone or an Instagram: export them to Google Contacts, then message them on WhatsApp, or open their Instagram from the list.

Find businesses

Three places to look. Everything found lands on the Leads tab, where only the ones that fit are shown.

Google Maps and Google

Pick a kind of business and where to search. Google Maps gives the most businesses, with phone numbers, and Google's Recently opened label on new ones. Google: business websites finds businesses through their own website, where the email usually is. Google: Instagram profiles finds Instagram accounts whose bio shows an email. Each search runs in a background tab at a human pace; keep the tabs open while it runs.

Areas

Businesses advertising their opening

A business that pays for an ad saying "grand opening" or "now open" just opened. Lead Hunter opens the Meta Ad Library in a tab, searches these words, and keeps the advertisers whose ad says so and who have no working website. Keep the tab on screen while it runs.

Instagram

New businesses often open an Instagram before anything else. Lead Hunter never searches or browses Instagram for you. When you come across a new business there, open its profile and press + Add to Lead Hunter (bottom right of the page). With the Instagram API set up in Settings, Lead Hunter then asks Meta for that account's bio, link and latest posts: an opening announcement, or an account that only started posting in the last few weeks, counts as proof that they just opened.

Intent Radar

People asking for a website or marketing help, owners announcing a business they are opening, owners saying business is slow, and companies hiring for marketing, from the last 14 days. Scan Reddit now opens Reddit in a tab (be logged in there) and reads your subreddits. For Facebook groups and LinkedIn (posts or jobs), open the page yourself and press Scan for leads at the bottom right. Sellers are filtered out. Until the AI check is set up every post is marked unverified: read it before you reach out.

Settings

Email check

Every address Lead Hunter finds is cleaned (typos like gmial.com or .con are fixed; broken, glued and placeholder addresses like @info@… are dropped), then its domain is checked: does it exist and receive email? That is free and always on, and nothing that fails it is ever exported.

Optional: a QuickEmailVerification key also checks that the exact mailbox exists (100 free a day). Without one, everything else still works.

Who fits

A business fits when it has no website, or its website is down, parked ("coming soon") or weak: not secure, not made for phones, or scoring under the bar below. A business with a good website does not fit. Businesses with proof they just opened (Google's own label, their own ad or Instagram post, a brand new Instagram, or your own say) come first, and the cold email file says "new business".

Areas

Every kind of business is searched in these areas, top to bottom, unless it has its own list. One per line, the way you would type it into Google Maps.

Kinds of business

One per kind of business, built in so there is nothing to import. Searches run as "search term area". An exclude word drops any business with it in its name or Google category (start a line with category: to only check the category). Leave Areas empty to use the list above. A listing Google marks as new is kept whatever its review count.

Checking businesses

Instagram

Lead Hunter never opens Instagram for this. It asks Meta's Instagram Graph API (Business Discovery) with your own token, and it can only see business and creator accounts. It reads their bio (often with their email), the link in it, and their latest posts, which say whether they just opened. You need an Instagram business or creator account linked to a Facebook page, and a Meta app. In the Graph API Explorer pick your app, add the permissions instagram_basic, instagram_manage_insights, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and business_management, press Generate Access Token, then run me/accounts?fields=instagram_business_account: the id it shows is your account id. Tokens from the Explorer stop working after about an hour; swap it for a long lived one (about 60 days) with the Access Token Debugger's Extend button. If the token stops working, lookups pause and you get an alert.

Intent Radar

Reddit refuses to show posts to anyone not logged in, and new Reddit API apps now need Reddit's approval. So Reddit is read in your own Reddit tab: press Scan Reddit now on the Intent tab (or Scan my subreddits at the bottom right of any Reddit page) while logged in to Reddit in this Chrome profile. It reads the newest posts of each subreddit below, 4 to 12 seconds apart. If you have an approved Reddit app, paste its id here and Reddit is scanned by itself every few hours instead. A block or "too many requests" pauses Reddit and alerts you.

Keywords: a post with any of these phrases is saved under that kind. Arabic phrases about websites or marketing count as those too.

AI check

An AI reads every post the keywords catch and says whether the person is really a buyer, what they need, what business they are and where, and how urgent it is. Posts it rules out are hidden (tick Show not buyers to see them). Use a free Google AI Studio key: aistudio.google.com > Get API key > Create API key. On Google's free tier there is a daily limit (the check waits and carries on when it is reached), and Google may use what is sent to improve its products; these are public posts. Or use a Claude key (console.anthropic.com > API keys). If both are filled in, Gemini is used. Keys stay in this browser and are only sent to Google or Anthropic.

Cold email

Lead Hunter finds the businesses and checks their emails. The cold email system on your Mac (cold-email/ in the repo) writes the emails, sends them, follows up and reads the replies. Press Export for cold email on the Leads tab and save the file in your RizcoReach Leads folder: it goes in within the hour. A business with proof that it just opened is marked "new business" in the file.

Saved

Your data

Leads live in this Chrome profile only. Back them up before removing the extension. Keys are left out of backups.

Problems by source

What went wrong lately on each site. A login wall, checkpoint or block pauses that source and shows an alert at the top of the Leads and Find tabs. Nothing tries to get past it.

Contacted memory

Everyone you mark contacted or export for cold email is remembered here, even after you delete the lead. Searches skip them and matching leads are hidden (tick Show contacted before to see them). Import your own list too: any CSV with phone numbers.
