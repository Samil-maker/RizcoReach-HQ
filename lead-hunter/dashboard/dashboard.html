Lead Hunter

Find businesses that are already spending on ads.

Lead Hunter pulls advertisers from the Meta Ad Library, looks for their website and a phone number, and fills in your website script for the ones with no working website.

  1. Start a hunt. Pick a niche (say, car detailing studios) and a country. A Facebook Ad Library tab opens and scrolls through the results for every keyword.
  2. Leads get checked automatically. Each advertiser's website is looked up and audited. Phone numbers come from WhatsApp ads, the website, and Google.
  3. Message the ones without a website. Open a lead and send the website script on WhatsApp, one bubble at a time. Advertisers with a working website are skipped.

New hunt

Lead Hunter opens the Meta Ad Library, searches each keyword, and scrolls until it has collected your target number of new advertisers. Keep the tab it opens on screen while it runs.

Niche edit the list in Settings

Only advertisers with active ads are collected. Everyone found is saved; the filters decide which ones count towards your target.

Tips

  • Any Ad Library page you browse is captured too, even without a hunt. Search, scroll, and the leads show up here.
  • Use 4 to 8 related keywords per niche. Different businesses use different words ("ceramic coating" finds people "car detailing" misses).
  • "Running at least 30 days" finds steady spenders with budget. "At most 14 days" finds businesses that just started advertising and may not have a good site yet.
  • Facebook may slow you down if you scroll thousands of ads in one go. A few hunts a day is plenty.

Phone queue

Send leads to your phone and work through them anywhere: each one opens WhatsApp on your phone with the message ready, and "sent" syncs back here. Without this you can still use the QR codes in each lead and in Phone mode.

  1. Press Generate a key, then Copy key.
  2. In Netlify: Site configuration → Environment variables → add LEAD_QUEUE_KEY with that key. Then Deploys → Trigger deploy.
  3. Press Save & test connection here.
  4. Scan the pairing code below with your phone and open the link. On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen so it opens like an app.

Scan with your phone's camera to open your outreach queue. Anyone with this code can see your queue, so don't share it.

Presets

One per kind of business. Searches run as "keyword area", one keyword at a time across every area, in the order you list them. Paste lists one item per line. An exclude word drops any business with it in its name or Google category. Start a line with category: to only check the category. The review range keeps small, owner run businesses: listings outside it are never opened or saved.

Scripts

Lead Hunter picks the script for each lead. No website, or a site that is down, gets the Website script. A website and no Meta ads gets the Ads script. Leads that run ads and have a working website are skipped. Variables: {{business}} {{niche}} {{city}} {{found_on}} {{website_line}} {{my_name}} {{agency}} {{my_phone}}

Email sender

Sends your emails from your own Gmail, even with Chrome closed. It starts with 15 a day and goes up over two weeks. It stops when someone replies and never emails anyone twice. WhatsApp is never sent for you.

The email sender uses the same key as the phone queue.

  1. Open script.google.com and sign in with the Gmail you want to send from.
  2. Click New project.
  3. Delete what's there and paste the script.
  4. Click + next to Services, pick Gmail API, then Add.
  5. Pick setup at the top and press Run, then Allow. Google warns the app isn't verified because it's your own script: click Advanced, then Go to.
  6. Done. It checks every 5 minutes and only sends Mon to Fri 9 to 6 Dubai time.

The first email is the lead's script in one message. The follow ups go as replies in the same thread. Same variables as the scripts.

Contacted memory

Everyone you mark contacted, message from the phone queue, email or export to Google Contacts is remembered here, even after you delete the lead. Searches skip them and matching leads are hidden (tick Show contacted before to see them). Import your own list too: any CSV with phone numbers, like your sheet of contacted numbers or Google Contacts > Export > Google CSV.
