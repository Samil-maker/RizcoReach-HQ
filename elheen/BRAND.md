# Elheen brand sheet

## The one idea

A brand is what people come to associate with a name after you deliver. The name
and logo are an empty container; results fill it. So the rules below are about
being simple and consistent, so every booking, message and page adds to the same
picture. (This is how Alex Hormozi frames branding: pair the name with outcomes,
again and again. These are his ideas applied to Elheen, not quotes.)

## Name

- **Elheen** (الحين). Gulf Arabic for "now". "احجز الحين" means "book now".
- Always lower-case in the logo: `elheen`. Capital E only at the start of a sentence.
- Domain: elheen.com. Instagram: @getelheen.

## What we are

The booking system for UAE service businesses whose clients come from Instagram,
WhatsApp and ads.

## Headline

**Turn enquiries into booked appointments.** Arabic sign-off: **احجز الحين**

## What we promise (and must be able to prove)

1. Your clients stay yours. We never show them a competitor.
2. Booking takes seconds, on the channels clients already use.
3. You can see which ad or post brought each booking.
4. One flat price per location.
5. A person in Dubai answers you on WhatsApp.

Never claim a number (bookings added, no-shows cut), a client logo or a review
until a real client's data backs it. The first three clients exist to earn those.

## Voice

- Plain words. Short sentences. No jargon.
- Speak to the owner as an equal: confident, warm, direct.
- Say what it does, then stop.

| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| "Clients book themselves" | "Seamless omnichannel scheduling" |
| "See which ad brought the booking" | "Advanced attribution analytics" |
| "Set up for you in a week" | "Frictionless onboarding" |

## Logo

- **The wordmark:** `elheen.` in Plus Jakarta Sans, medium weight, open and light,
  drawn as outlines so it looks the same everywhere.
- **The full stop** is the brand colour. It stands for "now": booked, done.
- **The icon:** `e.` on a pale orchid tile, for app icons and profile pictures.
- Clear space around the logo equals the height of the letter "e".
- Never stretch it, recolour the full stop, or put it on a busy photo.

## Colour

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Brand colour: buttons, links, the full stop | Orchid | `#A31A8A` |
| Hover and strong text on tint | Deep orchid | `#7E1269` |
| Dark sections | Aubergine | `#1F0A24` |
| Accent on dark backgrounds only | Pink | `#FF86D6` |
| Pale panel | Tint | `#F8E8F4` |
| Quiet panel | Soft | `#FAF5F8` |
| Main text | Text | `#1D1320` |
| Secondary text | Muted | `#65586A` |
| Lines | Line | `#E8DEE6` |
| Page | White | `#FFFFFF` |

Calendly is blue and Fresha is black with violet. Orchid sits between pink and
purple, so it is clearly Elheen's own and suits a beauty-led audience.

Colour is always flat. No gradients, glows or blurred shapes: those are what
make a site look machine-made.

## Type

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** for English, **Readex Pro** for Arabic. Both are free and
  stored in the site.
- Headings: weight 750, tight spacing, large.
- Body: weight 400 to 600.

## Shape and motion

- Rounded corners: 20px on cards, 36px on large panels, full pill on buttons.
- Generous white space. One idea per section.
- Simple line icons. No emoji as icons.
- Motion is calm and has a purpose: things rise into place once, the product
  demo plays, and the live dot pulses. Anyone who has asked their device for
  less motion gets a still page.

## What we take from the best booking sites, and what we don't

- Take: big confident headlines, lots of space, the product shown in use.
- Don't take: anyone's colours, wording, layouts or images.
