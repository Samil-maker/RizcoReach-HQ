# Elheen plan, 2026 to 2036

Written 14 November 2026. You are 17 and turn 18 on 16 December 2026. Ten years
from now is 2036, when you are 27.

Every number below is a target to aim at, not a forecast. Each stage has a gate.
If the gate is not met, fix the stage before spending more time or money.

## The rules that do not change

These apply Alex Hormozi's principles to Elheen. They are his ideas, not quotes.

1. **Sell before you build.** No feature is built until someone has paid for it or asked twice.
2. **One customer type at a time.** The product can serve many; the marketing serves one.
3. **Customers pay for growth.** The website fee and upfront months fund finding the next customer. No loans, no burning savings.
4. **Sell an outcome with a guarantee.** "Website and booking, live in 7 days, nothing upfront."
5. **Free channels first.** Outreach and content before paid ads.
6. **Keep customers before chasing more.** Do not scale while more than 3% leave each month.
7. **More, then better, then new.** Do more of what works before improving it, and improve it before adding anything new.
8. **RizcoReach stays the main business** until Elheen pays more than it does.

## Stage 1: First sales (now to January 2027)

- **Do:** 100 outreach messages a day offering "website + booking, nothing upfront" to salons, clinics and home improvement businesses.
- **Build:** only the landing page and a stopgap booking page that sends requests to the owner's WhatsApp.
- **Money in:** website fee (AED 749) plus the AED 480 monthly plan.
- **Money out:** the domain.
- **Gate:** 3 businesses buy. Whichever type buys first becomes the only type you market to.

## Stage 2: Version 1 (February to June 2027)

- **Do:** run the first clients by hand, write down every request and complaint.
- **Build:** the real calendar, staff, services, client list and WhatsApp reminders.
- **Also:** get your trade licence now that you are 18, so you can take payments.
- **Gate:** 10 businesses paying monthly, none leaving in 60 days, all bookings going through Elheen with none lost.

## Stage 3: A real product (July 2027 to end of 2028)

- **Do:** the same offer, more volume. Collect real numbers and turn each client into a case study.
- **Build:** deposits and payments, the "where did this booking come from" report, a self-serve signup.
- **Hire:** one support person once you cannot answer WhatsApp within the hour.
- **Target:** 100 paying locations. At about AED 300 a month each that is AED 30,000 a month.
- **Gate:** a new business can sign up and take its first booking without talking to you.

## Stage 4: Own the UAE niche (2029 to 2030)

- **Do:** add the second customer type. Let other marketing agencies resell Elheen to their clients.
- **Build:** a tool that imports clients from Fresha, an agency dashboard, mobile apps.
- **Target:** 500 to 1,000 locations, AED 150,000 to 300,000 a month.
- **Decide:** raise investment or stay self-funded. With these numbers you choose from strength.
- **Gate:** under 3% of customers leaving each month, and growth coming from referrals and agencies, not only from you.

## Stage 5: The Gulf (2031 to 2033)

- **Do:** open Saudi Arabia, the largest market in the region, then Qatar and Kuwait.
- **Build:** local payments and tax invoices for each country, a team in Riyadh.
- **Target:** 3,000 to 5,000 locations.
- **Risk:** Fresha and Rekaz are already there. Enter with the niche you already own, not with "everything for everyone".

## Stage 6: Platform (2034 to 2036)

- **Do:** earn from more than subscriptions: payments, and the ads service built into the product.
- **Target:** 10,000+ locations and AED 3 to 5 million a month.
- **Options at 27:** keep running it, sell part of it, or sell all of it.

For scale: Fresha took about 11 years to reach 130,000 businesses with roughly
$285 million of investor money. This plan aims for a far smaller, profitable
business built on customer money.

## What could kill it, and the answer

| Risk | Answer |
|---|---|
| Nobody buys in Stage 1 | Change the offer, not the product. Nothing has been built yet. |
| It goes down and a salon loses bookings | Few clients at first, daily backups, you answer WhatsApp yourself. |
| Fresha copies a feature | Sell the service and the local support, which they do not offer. |
| You spread across too many business types | Rule 2. One type until ten are paying. |
| RizcoReach suffers | Rule 8. Fixed hours a week for Elheen until Stage 3. |
| You run out of money | You cannot: the only upfront cost is the domain. |
