Questions

Before you ask.

Why set it up by hand?

Because a system only helps if it is set up right. We would rather spend 30 minutes with you than have you give up on day two.

What does it cost after the trial?

AED 79 a month, or AED 790 for a year. Founding businesses keep that price for as long as they stay. No commission and no charge per team member.

What if it is not for me?

Then you do not pay. The first 14 days are free and there is no card to cancel.

Can I keep working the way I do now?

Yes. Run Elheen beside your current way of working and switch fully when you are sure.

Who owns my client list?

You do. Download it as a spreadsheet at any time. We never contact your clients, and never show them another business.
