About

Elheen means "now".

الحين is how the Gulf says "right now". احجز الحين means "book now". That is the product, and the promise.

Why we are building it

Walk through any neighbourhood in Dubai and you will find salons, clinics and workshops doing excellent work, with a busy Instagram and no working website. Bookings happen over WhatsApp, in a notebook, or through an app that also advertises the business next door.

We think a business should own its clients, its brand and its calendar. Elheen gives it one link that clients book through, reminders that actually get read, and a clear view of what is working.

How we work

We sell before we build. Every feature exists because a real business asked for it. We set up our first customers by hand, we answer on WhatsApp, and we take no payment until the work is live.
