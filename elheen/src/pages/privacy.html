Privacy

Your data, in plain words.

Last updated 20 November 2026.

Who we are

Elheen is lead management software for service businesses in the UAE, at elheen.com. In this policy "we" means Elheen, "businesses" means the companies that use Elheen, and "clients" means the people who contact those businesses.

What we collect

From businesses: the business name, area, WhatsApp number, team member names, services, prices, quote terms, settings, and the passwords used to sign in (stored only as a secure hash). When a business pays for Elheen, the payment is handled by Ziina; we receive the result of the payment, never the card details.

From clients, on behalf of a business: what a client sends to that business through Elheen: their name, phone number, email, address or location, photos, messages, budget, preferred times, and their acceptance or decline of a quote. If a business connects its Facebook page, Instagram account, WhatsApp number or Google Ads lead forms, we also receive the lead-form answers and messages sent to that business through those channels, and the sender's name as shown by that platform. If a client arrives from an ad, we may keep the ad click ID so the business can see which ads bring work.

Automatically: basic technical information needed to run the service securely, such as the time of a request. We do not use advertising trackers on the app or enquiry pages.

Why we use it

Only to provide Elheen to the business: showing their leads, quotes, visits and payments, sending them alerts, writing follow-up messages for them to send, sending their replies when they choose to, and billing them for Elheen. We do not sell personal data, and we never contact a business's clients for our own purposes or show them another business.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run Elheen: Netlify (hosting and storage), Ziina (payments for Elheen), Meta (Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp, only when a business connects them), Google (only when a business connects Google Ads lead forms) and the providers that deliver phone notifications. Each receives only what it needs for its part. We may disclose data if UAE law requires it.

Who controls a client's data

The business a client contacted decides what happens to that client's details. Elheen stores and processes them for the business. Clients who want their details changed or removed can ask that business, or ask us and we will pass the request on.

How long we keep it

For as long as the business keeps its Elheen account. Deleting a lead in Elheen deletes its details, photos, quotes and messages. When a business closes its account, we delete its data within 30 days, apart from billing records we must keep by law.

How to delete your data

Businesses: delete any lead from its page in Elheen, disconnect Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp in Set-up at any time, or ask us to close your account and delete everything. You can download all your leads first from Set-up.

Clients, and people who connected through Facebook or Instagram: send us a message on Instagram at @getelheen with the business you contacted and the phone number, email or account you used. We confirm the deletion within 30 days.

Security

Connections are encrypted. Passwords are stored only as hashes. Access tokens for Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp are kept on our servers and never shown in the app. Each team member signs in with their own password and only sees what their role allows.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, we update the date above. Questions: message us on Instagram at @getelheen.
