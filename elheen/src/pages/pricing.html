Questions

About the price.

Is there a commission on jobs or bookings?

No. You keep everything your clients pay you. We charge AED 79 a month and nothing else.

Do I pay more as my team grows?

No. Add everyone who goes to site visits or takes appointments. The price stays the same.

What happens after the 14 free days?

Elheen asks you to pay by card, monthly or yearly. If you do not pay, your enquiry link keeps taking enquiries for you, and your leads open again as soon as you do.

How do I pay?

By card inside the app, through Ziina, a UAE payment provider. Prefer a bank transfer? Message us and we will add it to your account.

What if it is not worth it?

Then you do not pay. The first 14 days are free with no card, and if your first paid month does not feel worth AED 79, tell us and we refund it in full.

Is there a contract?

No. Pay month by month or for a year, and stop whenever you like. You can download all your leads as a spreadsheet at any time.
