Questions

About the price.

Is there a commission on bookings?

No. You keep everything your clients pay you. We charge the monthly price and nothing else.

Do I pay more as my team grows?

No. The price is per location, however many people work there.

What if I have more than one location?

Each location has its own page and calendar, at the same price each. Tell us if you have three or more and we will agree a group price.

Can I change plan later?

Yes, at any time. You can start on Start and move to Grow when you want the assistant.

Is there a contract?

No. You pay month by month and can stop whenever you like. Your client list is yours to take with you.
