VAT since 2018. Corporate Tax since 2023. Mandatory e-invoicing phasing in from 2026. Missing any of it means real FTA penalties — starting at AED 1,000 and climbing fast.

VAT filings, every quarter

Registered businesses must calculate and file VAT accurately — with correct TRNs, rates, and invoice formatting — or risk fines.

Corporate Tax, every year

The UAE is phasing in mandatory structured e-invoicing for B2B and B2G transactions. Businesses need to be format-ready.
